EVERYDAY IDIOMS
1No worries
کوئی بات نہیں / کوئی پریشانی نہیں
Mean:It's okay / no problem
Ex:"No worries, I can wait."
Easy:"You forgot your book? No worries, you can share mine."
Adv:"No worries about the minor delay; we have built sufficient buffer time into the project timeline."
2Sounds good
اچھی بات ہے / منظور ہے
Mean:I agree with the plan
Ex:"Let's meet at 6. — Sounds good!"
Easy:"Pizza for dinner? Sounds good to me!"
Adv:"Your proposal to restructure the department sounds good, provided we can secure executive approval first."
3Give it a shot
کوشش کرنا / آزمانا
Mean:Try something
Ex:"I've never tried skiing, but I'll give it a shot."
Easy:"I don't know if I can fix it, but I'll give it a shot."
Adv:"The algorithm seems complex, but give it a shot before we outsource the entire module."
4Call it a day
آج کا کام ختم کرنا / دن کا کام ختم کرنا
Mean:Stop working for today
Ex:"We've been working for hours. Let's call it a day."
Easy:"I'm tired. Let's call it a day and finish tomorrow."
Adv:"After resolving the critical server issue, the IT team decided to call it a day and resume routine maintenance in the morning."
5Hang in there
ہمت نہ ہارنا / ثابت قدم رہنا
Mean:Don't give up
Ex:"I know it's difficult, but hang in there."
Easy:"The test is hard, but hang in there! You can do it!"
Adv:"Despite the volatile market conditions, investors were advised to hang in there and avoid panic selling."
6It's up to you
یہ آپ کی مرضی ہے / آپ فیصلہ کر سکتے ہیں
Mean:You can decide
Ex:"We can eat here or go somewhere else. It's up to you."
Easy:"Which movie should we watch? It's up to you!"
Adv:"The board has outlined both strategies, but the final decision is up to you as the project lead."
7My bad
میری غلطی / میری خطا
Mean:My mistake
Ex:"Oh, I sent you the wrong file. My bad!"
Easy:"I took your pen by mistake. My bad!"
Adv:"My bad for overlooking the clause in the contract; I'll have the legal team amend it immediately."
8I'm good
نہیں شکریہ / میں ٹھیک ہوں
Mean:No, thank you / I'm okay
Ex:"Would you like another coffee? — No, I'm good, thanks."
Easy:"More water? No, I'm good, thanks."
Adv:"The host offered another round of dessert, but I'm good; I've already exceeded my calorie limit for the day."
9On the same page
ایک ہی صفحے پر ہونا / ایک ہی رائے ہونا
Mean:Have the same understanding
Ex:"Let's make sure we're on the same page before we start."
Easy:"Are we on the same page about the homework assignment?"
Adv:"Before proceeding with the merger, both companies wanted to ensure they were on the same page regarding intellectual property rights."
10Keep an eye on
نظر رکھنا / دھیان دینا
Mean:Watch/check something
Ex:"Can you keep an eye on my bag for a minute?"
Easy:"Please keep an eye on the baby while I cook."
Adv:"The analyst was asked to keep an eye on the fluctuating exchange rates and report any significant movements to the treasury department."
