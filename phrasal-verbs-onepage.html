PHRASAL VERBS
1Come over
کسی کے گھر جانا / کسی سے ملنے جانا
Mean:Visit someone at their home
Ex:"Why don't you come over for coffee?"
Easy:"Can you come over to my house after school?"
Adv:"The manager asked the client to come over to the office for a detailed discussion regarding the merger proposal."
2Go out
گھر سے باہر تفریح کے لیے جانا
Mean:Leave home for a social activity
Ex:"We're going out for dinner tonight."
Easy:"I like to go out with my friends on weekends."
Adv:"After months of isolation, she finally decided to go out and reconnect with her professional network at the industry gala."
3Wake up
نیند سے بیدار ہونا
Mean:Stop sleeping
Ex:"I usually wake up at 7 a.m."
Easy:"Please wake up early tomorrow for the trip."
Adv:"The nation needed to wake up to the harsh realities of climate change before irreversible damage was done."
4Get up
بستر سے اٹھنا
Mean:Leave your bed
Ex:"I got up late this morning."
Easy:"I get up at 6 o'clock every day for school."
Adv:"Despite the exhaustion from the overnight flight, she had to get up and prepare for the crucial board meeting."
5Turn on
کسی آلہ یا بتی کو چالو کرنا
Mean:Start a device/light
Ex:"Can you turn on the lights?"
Easy:"Please turn on the TV, my favorite show is starting."
Adv:"The engineer was instructed to turn on the backup generators before the main power grid completely failed."
6Turn off
کسی آلہ یا بتی کو بند کرنا
Mean:Stop a device/light
Ex:"Please turn off the TV."
Easy:"Don't forget to turn off the fan when you leave."
Adv:"The security protocol required them to turn off all electronic devices before entering the classified briefing room."
7Put on
کپڑے یا زیورات پہننا
Mean:Wear clothing/accessories
Ex:"Put on your jacket. It's cold outside."
Easy:"Put on your shoes before going outside."
Adv:"She put on her most confident demeanor before stepping onto the stage to deliver the keynote address."
8Take off
کپڑے اتارنا / زمین چھوڑنا
Mean:Remove clothing / leave the ground
Ex:"You can take off your shoes at the door."
Easy:"Please take off your hat inside the classroom."
Adv:"The aircraft began to take off as the passengers fastened their seatbelts for the long-haul flight to Tokyo."
9Look after
کسی یا کسی چیز کی دیکھ بھال کرنا
Mean:Take care of someone/something
Ex:"Can you look after my dog this weekend?"
Easy:"My mother asked me to look after my little sister."
Adv:"The curator was entrusted to look after the priceless artifacts during the museum's extensive renovation period."
10Call back
کسی کے فون کا جواب دینا / واپس کال کرنا
Mean:Return someone's phone call
Ex:"I'm busy right now. Can I call you back?"
Easy:"The doctor will call back with your test results."
Adv:"The embassy promised to call back within 24 hours regarding the visa application status."
